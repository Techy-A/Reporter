# Leave Policy

Applies to all full-time employees. Version 2026-01.

## 4.1 Casual leave
Employees get 8 days of casual leave per calendar year, credited on 1 January. Casual leave cannot be
carried forward to the next year and is not encashed.

## 4.2 Sick leave
Employees get 6 days of sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave and casual leave can be clubbed together
for a maximum of 5 consecutive working days. A medical certificate is needed for more than 2 consecutive
days of sick leave.

## 4.3 Earned leave
Earned leave accrues at 1.5 days per completed month of service. Up to 30 days can be carried forward.
Earned leave must be applied for at least 7 days in advance.

## 4.4 Work from home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with their manager's approval in Keka. WFH is not
allowed during the first 30 days after joining.

## 5.1 Overtime credit
Overtime approved by the reporting manager is credited with the next month's salary.

## 6.1 Notice period and buyout
The notice period is 60 days after confirmation. Employees may buy out the remaining notice period by paying
basic salary for the unserved days, subject to HR approval.

## 7.1 Mandatory trainings
POSH awareness and information security trainings are mandatory every year and must be completed in the LMS
within 30 days of being assigned.
