# Competitive Landscape, Pricing and GTM: AI HR Assistants / Employee Self-Service Agents (India focus)

Research date: 6 Nov 2026. About 24 searches and fetches. Most sources are vendor press releases or third-party aggregators. Pricing figures from aggregators conflict and are flagged. Anything dated before 2025 is marked [possibly stale].

## Q1. What AI/agent features do incumbents and new entrants offer? Channels? Do they execute or only answer?

### Takeaway
By late 2026 the Indian SMB/mid-market HRMS vendors had shipped "execute, not just answer" agents inside their own products, and they bundle them at no extra cost. greytHR NAVOS (June 2026, included in all paid plans) and Kredily KAI (Aug 2026, 110+ skills) are the clearest examples, and Zimyo 3.0 is described as agentic. Darwinbox has a Slack/Teams Super Agent and an MCP server. Global enterprise players (Workday, SAP, ServiceNow-Moveworks, Leena AI) are consolidating through acquisitions and price for 1,000+ seat enterprises, not 50–500 employee firms.

### Inferences
- In India, "agentic HR ops assistant inside the HRMS" became table stakes in 2026 for the vendors with the most 50–500 employee customers (greytHR, Kredily, Zimyo). Bundling it free removes the obvious standalone price point for in-HRMS leave, attendance, and payroll actions.
- Channel coverage looks like the main white space. The Indian incumbents' agents appear to be in-app (web and mobile). Only Darwinbox has confirmed Slack/Teams, and I found no confirmed WhatsApp-native HR agent from any major vendor. Many Indian 50–500 firms (manufacturing, retail, field staff, blue and grey collar) rely on WhatsApp.
- Cross-system execution is the other gap. Darwinbox's Super Agent pitch (HR + IT + Finance in one action) is the enterprise version. For SMBs, the equivalent spans HRMS + Google Workspace/M365 + Slack + payroll/CA + expense tools. Single-HRMS agents do not cover it.

### Gaps
- No primary-source confirmation of Keka AI features. Keka's newsroom was not retrieved.
- Rezolve.ai and Moveworks channel and feature specifics were not verified this session. From general knowledge, both are Teams/Slack-native and execute IT and HR actions, but this needs confirmation.
- No confirmed WhatsApp HR agent from any incumbent. This is an absence of evidence, not proof.
- The Amber/inFeedo 2025–2026 roadmap is unverified.

## Q2. Pricing: PEPM in INR/USD for Indian HRMS and AI add-ons; deal sizes for 50–500 employee firms

### Takeaway
Indian HRMS costs about ₹45–₹300 PEPM, often as a base fee plus a per-head charge. AI agents are increasingly bundled at ₹0 extra in India (greytHR). Global AI service agents are priced for enterprises: Leena AI at about $150/user/year with a 1,000-seat minimum, SAP on metered AI Units. A 50–500 employee Indian firm spends roughly ₹0.3–9 lakh a year on HRMS, which caps what it will pay for an overlay.

### Cited Findings
- **greytHR** (India calculator): Essential ₹2,495/month + ₹45/employee above 50; Growth ₹4,495 + ₹85/employee above 50; Premium custom; excludes tax. Add-ons are charged per user per module or PEPM. TrustRadius lists conflicting figures. — [greytHR price calculator](https://www.greythr.com/pricing-calculator/); [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com/products/greythr/pricing). GCC pricing is $50/month + $2 PEPM above 10 employees. — [greytHR KSA calculator](https://www.greythr.com/saudi-arabia/pricing-calculator/)
- **NAVOS AI** costs ₹0 extra on paid greytHR plans. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/spotlight/news-wire/greythr-launches-navos-agentic-ai-that-turns-hr-intent-into-action)
- **Keka** (from a competitor, HROne, 2026): Foundation ₹9,999/month, Strength ₹12,999, Growth ₹15,999, with ₹90 / ₹120 / ₹150 per extra employee. An alternative reading is Foundation ₹9,999 flat up to 100 employees, then ₹85 PEPM. Older listings show ₹6,999 + ₹60. Keka's official pages say "contact sales". - **Zoho Zia Agents**: free to build; pay for model usage via prepaid credits (1,000 credits per USD) or bring your own key (third-party). — [Layer3Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/zoho-zia-explained)

### Inferences (illustrative deal sizes, computed from the list prices above, not sourced deal data)
- greytHR Growth, 100 employees: ₹4,495 + 50 × ₹85 = about ₹8,745/month, or about ₹1.05 lakh/year (~$1.2K).
- greytHR Growth, 500 employees: ₹4,495 + 450 × ₹85 = about ₹42,745/month, or about ₹5.1 lakh/year (~$6K).
- Keka Strength, 300 employees: at the HROne figures, roughly ₹3–4.4 lakh/year depending on how the base is read.
- An overlay agent priced like Rezolve (about $3 PEPM ≈ ₹250) would cost as much as, or more than, the customer's entire HRMS (₹45–150 PEPM). That is a hard sell when incumbents bundle agents free.
- Plausible overlay price points:
  - ₹40–100 PEPM; or
  - a flat platform fee of about ₹10–25K/month for up to 200 employees; or
  - outcome or usage pricing (per resolved request or per workflow run), echoing SAP's per-action metering and Leena's resolution guarantee.
- The ACV for a 50–500 employee company is likely ₹1–6 lakh/year ($1.2–7K). That pushes toward low-touch, partner-led, or product-led selling rather than field sales.

### Gaps

- I found no actual transaction or deal-size data for Indian HR SaaS in the 50–500 segment.

## Q3. Gaps and complaints about current HR bots

### Takeaway
The recurring complaints are generic or wrong answers, dead ends with no human handoff, handoffs that lose context, and poor handling of sensitive or emotional queries. HR teams also distrust bot accuracy. Firsthand Reddit or G2 review text was not retrieved this session.

### Cited Findings
- Capterra survey: 49% of HR employees at firms with an HR chatbot have received at least one complaint about it. 94% rate their bot good or excellent, but only 67% are completely confident it gives accurate information. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/resources/hr-chatbot-research/) [date not confirmed; possibly pre-2025]
- A handoff that drops an employee into a generic inbox with no history "is worse than no assistant at all". Rising deflection together with rising repeat contacts means employees are being "brushed off". — [Rework](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/ai-for-hr/ai-employee-self-service)
- Employees who can't get accurate answers go back to contacting HR directly, which increases transactional volume. — [peopleHum glossary](https://www.peoplehum.com/glossary/hr-chatbot)
- A generic bot reply to an employee in distress signals that the organisation does not care. — [peopleHum](https://www.peoplehum.com/blog/will-hr-chatbots-empathise-better-than-humans)
- High escalation rates indicate knowledge gaps or poor intent recognition. — [Hyring](https://hyring.com/free-hr-toolkit/hr-glossary/chatbot-hr)
- Structural gaps for SMBs: enterprise AI service desks have 1,000-seat floors, 1–2 year terms, and $50K–200K implementation fees. eesel notes this excludes companies with about 800 employees. — [eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/fr/blog/tarifs-leena-ai)
- Keka was rated 8/100 for agent-readiness, which suggests limited APIs for third-party agents. — [xpay](https://www.xpay.sh/saas-pricing/keka/)
- Darwinbox's Super Agent was limited to design partners at launch. — [Reworked](https://www.reworked.co/the-wire/darwinbox-launches-super-agent-an-ai-teammate-for-every-employee-that-gets-work-done/)

### Inferences
- Gaps an Indian SMB-focused entrant can credibly target:
  1. WhatsApp and vernacular access for non-desk staff.
  2. A reliable human handoff with full context into the HR team's queue.
  3. Policy answers grounded in the company's own handbook, with citations.
  4. Cross-tool execution beyond one HRMS: onboarding and offboarding across Google/M365, Slack, laptops, and payroll vendors.
  5. Accuracy and audit guarantees.
- Incumbent in-HRMS agents mostly address HR-admin productivity (greytHR's "5X HR bandwidth" message) more than the employee-facing experience across channels.

### Gaps
- No firsthand G2, Capterra, or Reddit review quotes on Keka, greytHR, Darwinbox, or Leena bots were collected. A targeted review scrape is still needed.
- No data yet on NAVOS or KAI accuracy or user satisfaction; both are too new.

## Q4. GTM: how Indian HR SaaS sells, sales cycles, pilots, procurement requirements

### Takeaway
Indian SMB HR SaaS sells through inside sales, reseller/channel partners, and CA-firm and MSME-scheme distribution (Kredily lets CA firms extend promos to clients). Security badges are standard: SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and GDPR are displayed by greytHR. DPDP obligations bind from 13 May 2027, which is a near-term procurement driver. I found no hard data on sales-cycle length.

### Inferences
- For 50–500 employee buyers, the decision-maker is usually the HR head, founder, or CFO, not a CHRO with a procurement team. Expect:
  - a 2–6 week cycle for self-serve or inside sales;
  - a free 30-day pilot on 1–2 workflows (leave, attendance, policy Q&A, onboarding);
  - a security questionnaire instead of a full audit.
  This is an inference; no sourced cycle data was found.
- The DPDP May 2027 deadline is a selling point and a requirement. An overlay agent processes employee personal data, so it needs:
  - a DPA;
  - data residency in India;
  - consent and notice flows;
  - role-based access that mirrors the HRMS;
  - audit logs;
  - deletion on request.
  ISO 27001 is achievable early; SOC 2 Type II takes 6–12 months.
- Partner channels worth testing: CA, payroll-outsourcing, and PEO firms (Kredily is already doing this), HRMS resellers, and the Slack/Teams/Zoho marketplaces.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on sales-cycle length, CAC, win rates, CHRO-community GTM (for example People Matters or SHRM India events), or Keka/Darwinbox app marketplaces for third-party agents. I could not confirm that Keka or greytHR run public third-party app marketplaces.

## Q5. Is an overlay agent on top of an existing HRMS a viable wedge, or will HRMS vendors add it themselves?

### Takeaway
The evidence points strongly toward HRMS vendors building it themselves and bundling it free (greytHR, Kredily, Zimyo, Darwinbox, Workday, SAP), with large acquisitions consolidating the standalone category (Moveworks to ServiceNow for $2.85B; Sana and Paradox to Workday). A pure in-HRMS Q&A and transaction overlay is weak. A wedge is viable only where incumbents are structurally absent: multi-system workflows, WhatsApp and vernacular channels, HRMS-agnostic deployment for firms on Keka or other HRMS lacking agents, and done-for-you HR ops (managed service plus agent).
